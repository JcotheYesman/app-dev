# My Favorite Movie 🐉

## How to Train Your Dragon

**How to Train Your Dragon** is an animated adventure movie released in 2010. It tells the story of *Hiccup*, a young Viking who becomes friends with a dragon named Toothless, even though his village believes dragons are dangerous.

### 📖 Movie Information

- **Title:** How to Train Your Dragon
- **Release Year:** 2010
- **Genre:** Animation, Action, Adventure, Fantasy
- **Main Characters:** Hiccup and Toothless
- **Director:** Chris Sanders and Dean DeBlois

### ⭐ Why I Like This Movie

I like this movie because of its **beautiful animation, exciting adventures, and emotional story**. My favorite part is the friendship between Hiccup and Toothless because it shows that understanding others is better than judging them based on fear.

> “A story about friendship, courage, and understanding.”

### 🎯 My Favorite Things About the Movie

1. The special bond between Hiccup and Toothless.
2. The amazing dragon designs and flying scenes.
3. The lesson about friendship, trust, and acceptance.
